Privacy Policy

Family Logistics Agent

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Overview

Family Logistics Agent is a privately operated application designed to support family scheduling, communication, reminders, and coordination. The application is intended for personal and family use only and is not offered as a public or commercial service.

2. Information Accessed

With authorization from the account holder, the application may access:

  • Gmail messages and associated metadata using read-only permissions.
  • Google Calendar events and scheduling information.
  • Information from other family-related services connected by the account holder.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Information is processed solely to provide family-related scheduling, notifications, summaries, and automation.

Google user data is not used for advertising, marketing, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Processing and Storage

The application operates through privately managed automation infrastructure, including a self-hosted n8n installation.

Information may be processed or stored as needed to perform configured workflows. Retention depends on the relevant workflow and connected services.

5. Data Sharing

Information is not sold to third parties. Data may be transmitted to connected service providers when necessary to perform configured functions, subject to the account holder's selected integrations.

6. Security

Access to Google services is authorized through Google's OAuth authentication system. The application uses permissions granted by the account holder and applies reasonable technical safeguards to protect information.

7. Access and Revocation

Users can revoke access through their Google Account security settings. Questions regarding personal information can be directed to the application operator.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the application operator through the contact information provided during authorization.
